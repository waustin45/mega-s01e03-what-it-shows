The three problems

parser: day and month swap: 379 reports · ticket #4

The shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so 7 April is stored as 4 July. Only days 1 to 12 can swap, so most bookings look fine and the rest land months away. These are reports from day-first tenants by app, by email, or through a booking-v2 form, counted as the desk files them by tenant, so the one-day moves below are in this count too.

hint: the form tells people to type month first: 33 reports · ticket #2

booking-v3 prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field and reads what is typed the tenant’s way, day first. People do what the hint says and get a swapped date before the parser sees it. These are the reports that came through the form at the booking-v3 tenants. The same tenants’ app and email reports are parser. Fix the parser alone and these stay broken.

one-day move: the date moves by one day, it does not swap: 5 reports · ticket #3

The desk read these as a one-day move already (desk-notes.json), and the bodies say so: Tuesday became Monday, Lisbon and Toronto saw different days for one meeting, June was one day off, an invite came before its booking. A swap cannot do that. It looks like time zone or daylight-saving handling. The desk counts them under parser by tenant, and so does this page; the parser fix will not fix them, so they have their own ticket.

How the split is made

  • One id, one report: the export repeats some reports, so they are counted once (uniqueById).
  • The tenant decides the cause, not intake’s tag (causes.ts). Intake guesses from the reporter’s browser and is wrong in both directions; the “tagged as” column shows by how much.
  • Counts are the desk’s own view: groupByCause and readAs from causes.ts, as CauseTable uses them (split.ts).
  • The desk’s own notes decide the one-day moves (desk-notes.json).
  • Every number on this page is computed from reports-4471.json when the page renders.

Lessons

  • A matching subject line says two people are unhappy about the same thing, not that one bug did it. Read the body and the way in before you merge.
  • When a note on the desk contradicts the table, the table is wrong: give the note its own row.
