Triage: duplicate reports

owner support desk proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Keep one ticket per evidenced cause, with a count of unique reports. A matching subject helps find a candidate ticket; subject alone is not a cause.

This guidance follows proposed decision 0002, which Austin must review. Accepted decision 0001 is preserved as history; this proposal does not record owner approval.

The proposed rule

  1. Read the body and identify the tenant, its locale, booking form and report channel.
  2. Compare that evidence with the candidate ticket’s cause. For #4471, a day-first tenant’s booking-v3 form reports belong to hint; app, email and booking-v2 form reports belong to parser. Intake’s browser-based tag is a guess, not the tenant’s cause.
  3. Merge only when both reports have the same known cause, as sameCause checks in causes.ts. Different subjects or tenants can still share a cause. The same subject or tenant can cover different causes when the form or channel differs.
  4. Keep different causes apart. If a known cause has no ticket, open one. Count each report ID once.

When you are unsure

Keep the report separate for review. Record missing tenant, locale, form or channel evidence, or a body that does not fit the known explanation. An unknown cause is not a match, including when both reports are unknown. Do not merge on subject or intake tag to clear the queue.

Keep observations attached to their report IDs without inventing a new cause. The one-day observations in #4471 remain included in the parser count; their root cause and the parser fix’s effect on them are unverified.
