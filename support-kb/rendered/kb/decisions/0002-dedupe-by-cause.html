0002 · Match duplicates by evidenced cause

owner Brandon proposed reviewed 2026-10-28 · 0 days ago

Proposed: 28 October 2026 · Author: Brandon · Review requested: Austin

Context and evidence

Decision 0001 accepted subject-only merging. #4471 shows why that loses information: matching subjects combined parser and booking-form hint reports, with different engineering outcomes.

The 2026-10-04 export’s causes.ts classifies by tenant locale, form and channel, not subject or the browser-based intake tag. sameCause requires a known cause on both sides, and the triage explorer already uses it. The engineering update, checked 2026-10-28, records #4471 closed 2026-10-05: parser FIXED, hint WON’T FIX because booking-v3 was retired. The incident page records the ticket dispositions.

Proposed replacement for 0001

Use a subject match to find candidates, then read the report and compare cause evidence. For this incident, day-first tenant reports through the booking-v3 form are hint; app, email and booking-v2 form reports are parser. Merge only when both causes are known and match. Different subjects can share a cause; matching subjects can describe different causes. Count unique report IDs, preserving the original export and its classification.

If accepted by the owner, this record replaces the subject-only rule in 0001. Until that review, this is a proposal, not an accepted policy or a claim of owner approval. Leave 0001 unchanged. The proposed triage guidance describes the steps.

Uncertain cases

Keep unknown causes separate for desk review, including two unknowns. Record the missing tenant, locale, form or channel evidence and any contradictory body text. Do not use subject or intake tag as a substitute. Preserve one-day observations without promoting them to a verified timezone/DST cause or adding their reports to the total again.

Consequences

  • Triage takes more reading and may leave reports pending evidence.
  • Engineering gets separate threads for different evidenced causes, even when subjects match.
  • Unusual observations remain visible within the desk’s counts. A duplicate disposition does not prove the parser fix cured them, or that it cannot cure them.
  • Future policy changes get new records; accepted decisions remain historical records.
