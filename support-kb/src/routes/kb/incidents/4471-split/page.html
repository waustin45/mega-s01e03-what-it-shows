How the split is made

  • One ID, one report: uniqueById removes identical export repeats.
  • groupByCause in the original causes.ts uses tenant locale, form and channel. Intake’s browser-based tag is a guess; the table keeps it visible for comparison.
  • split.ts keeps the cause totals separate from overlapping desk-note observations.
  • Counts come from the original report export and tenant directory when the page renders. Historical timestamps and classification are unchanged.
  • Ticket dispositions were checked on 2026-10-28; they and the engineering update are later evidence, not fields in the 2026-10-04 export.

Lessons
